  Punjab has emerged as a leader in wastewater reuse by moving beyond policy to large-scale implementation. This film **Farmers First** *for used water* documents how treated wastewater is conveyed from treatment plants to agricultural fields through dedicated pipeline networks, providing a reliable source of irrigation water for thousands of farmers.At the heart of this initiative is a participatory approach that engages farmer committees in the planning, implementation, operation, and maintenance of pipelines and pumping systems. The program has enabled the productive reuse of treated wastewater, reduced pressure on freshwater resources, and helped curb excessive groundwater extraction.
  
  Through stories from the field, this film showcases how strong policy, public investment, and community participation can work together to create sustainable water solutions for agriculture.
