National Workshop on Post-Disaster Housing Recovery, Reconstruction and Resettlement, was held in Kozhikode, Kerala, from 11–13 November 2026, bringing together government officials from various states to discuss strengthening institutional arrangements for housing recovery. On behalf of PiC, I presented on the role of Housing Reconstruction Resource Centres (HRRCs) in supporting owner-driven housing reconstruction, emphasising that financial assistance alone cannot ensure safe and resilient houses. Drawing on our experiences from the Gujarat earthquake (2001), Kosi floods (2008) and Nepal earthquake (2015), I highlighted the importance of socio-technical facilitation that enables homeowners with the knowledge, technical guidance and administrative support needed to take ownership of the reconstruction process. Ms. Rita Missal (Member, NDMA) led the workshop. Ms. Sweety Changsan (ACS, Govt. of Assam), Ms. Tinku Biswal (Principal Secretary, Revenue and Disaster Management, Govt. of Kerala), Dr. Radhakrishnan Jaganathan (Chairperson, TNERC), Dr. Sekar Kuriakose (Member Secretary, KSDMA), etc. were other prominant participants bringing wide experiences of effective recovery from past disasters.
