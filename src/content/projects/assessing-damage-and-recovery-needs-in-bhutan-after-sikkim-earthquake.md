The Sikkim earthquake of September 18, 2011 affected India, Nepal and Bhutan, causing significant losses. At the request of the Royal Government of Bhutan to the UN, Vivek Rawal of People in Centre led the Joint Rapid Assessment mission of the RGoB, the UN and the World Bank to assess the damage, losses and recovery needs. An inter-disciplinary team including UNDP-BCPR, UNOCHA, UNDP Bhutan, the World Bank and various ministries and departments of the Government of Bhutan undertook the assessment in the first two weeks of October 2011. Photographs of the damage can be seen [here](https://picasaweb.google.com/101554552104048172409/SikkimEarthquake2011Bhutan?authuser=0&authkey=Gv1sRgCNS995f6hvanqQE&feat=directlink).
