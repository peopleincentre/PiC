In continuation of its engagement with marketplaces, People in Centre helped in re-organising vegetable market at Shardaben hospital. The Saraspur marketplace was in existance for more than 40 years but there was no expansion, despite increase in number of vendors over the years. Presently, there was added pressure on the market due to road expansipon. An alternative plan was developed under the guidance of Rushank Mehta by Ms. Priyanka Kulkarni, a student of CEPT. All the existing vendors were accommodated in an open space nearby, allocated by AMC for the vendors during their negotiations with SEWA. Appropriate infrastructure was provided for the market. It was proposed that the vendors also take care of the adjoining garden and develop it as food market.
