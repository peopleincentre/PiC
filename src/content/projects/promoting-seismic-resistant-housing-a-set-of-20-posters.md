People in Centre supported a campaign to improve seismic resistance in existing houses, undertaken by UNNATI – Organization for Development Education in Kutch district, Gujarat. A set of 20 posters was prepared on how homeowners could improve the seismic resistance of their existing houses, focusing on simple principles that could ensure much improved performance through simple retrofitting measures. The set of posters is available [here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/20posters-PiC.pdf).
