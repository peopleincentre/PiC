Alka Palrecha from People in Centre has been leading studies on wastewater use. Policy frameworks promote reuse but do not pay adequate attention to rural users, and there are increasing contestations between urban and rural users over access rights to wastewater. An article published by India Water Portal can be accessed at [this link](https://www.indiawaterportal.org/articles/whose-wastewater-it).
