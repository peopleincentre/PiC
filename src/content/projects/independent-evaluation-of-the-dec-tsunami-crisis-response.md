People in Centre participated in the Independent Evaluation of the DEC Tsunami Crisis Response, with a team-member responsibility for evaluating DEC-supported programmes in India. The report identified critical areas for learning in such large-scale humanitarian responses to make them more effective. The report is available in the public domain [here](http://www.reliefweb.int/library/documents/2005/dec-sas-31dec.pdf).
