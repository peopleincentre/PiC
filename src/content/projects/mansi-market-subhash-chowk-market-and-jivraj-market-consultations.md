Due to implementation of BRTS, the local buses were rerouted through the marketplace of Mansi. Around 100 street vendors selling vegetables were asked to evacuate. Sewa asked **People in Centre** to evaluate the traffic and vending situation. A quick consultation and area analysis were carried out to enable the street vendors to negotiate with the authorities to allow them to carry out their business while not obstructing the new traffic route. Similar consultations were carried out with street vendors at Subhash Chowk marketplace and Jivrajpark marketplace with vendors who were facing problems due to road widening and other projects being implemented by the authorities.
