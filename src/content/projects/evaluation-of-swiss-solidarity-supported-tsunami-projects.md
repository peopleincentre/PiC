People in Centre evaluated tsunami projects in India supported by an aid of 25 million Swiss francs from Swiss Solidarity, in partnership with P. Thamizoli. The projects were implemented by Swiss NGOs and their Indian partners in Tamil Nadu, Pondicherry and Kerala. The report was submitted to Swiss Solidarity, and an evaluation workshop to discuss the findings and recommendations was organised on October 2–4, 2008 in Trivandrum. Though the complete report is not in the public domain, a brief article on the evaluation workshop is available on the Swiss Solidarity website — you may access it [here](https://www.swiss-solidarity.org/Asia-Quake.57.0.html?&L=3&tx_ttnews%5Btt_news%5D=3257&tx_ttnews%5BbackPid%5D=55&cHash=1d2df1cb82). The photo gallery related to the workshop is [here](https://www.swiss-solidarity.org/Photo-galleries-2009.407.0.html?&L=3&tx_chgallery_pi1%5Bdir%5D=2&cHash=4991bbcae2#c2789).
