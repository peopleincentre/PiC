The response to the tsunami indicated many aspects where there is a critical need to learn. This article, by Binoy Acharya, Vivek Rawal and Vinish Kathuria, explores these gap areas and identifies the critical learning required to make the response more effective and people-centric. This critical review of the post-tsunami response is available [here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/Tsunami_article.pdf).
