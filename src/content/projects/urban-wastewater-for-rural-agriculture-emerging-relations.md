The case studies of various municipal towns of Gujarat indicate the emerging relationship patterns around disposal and usage of sewage water by farmers in peri-urban villages. Alka Palrecha from ‘People in Centre’ Consulting was invited to Excreta Does Matter: Anil Agarwal Dialogue-2 organised by Centre for Science and Environment, New Delhi on March 4-5, 2013. The presentation on the subject can be seen [here](http://www.cseindia.org/userfiles/alka_palrecha.pdf) and the case studies can be downloaded [here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/Case-studies-from-Gujarat.pdf)
