**People in Centre** has long been engaged with issues of marketplaces and street vending in Ahmedabad, focusing on existing marketplaces in both the old and expanded city limits. Because these require a different strategy to be supported as livelihood-generation spaces for the poor, People in Centre — after working on the Manek Chowk and Teen Darwaja markets — collaborated with SEWA on other marketplaces, including the redevelopment of markets near Danapith (Rajnagar), the State Transport (ST) bus-stand area and the Jamalpur vending markets, accommodating existing street sellers. The basic plans can be [viewed here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B-_-328lImDicGpZUjVfSEdLQUU/view?usp=sharing).
