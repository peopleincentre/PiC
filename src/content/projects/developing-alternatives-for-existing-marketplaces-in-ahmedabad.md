**People in Centre** has been involved with issues of marketplaces and street vending in Ahmedabad. Many of the areas we have been focusing are existing marketplaces located in the old and expanded city limits of Ahmedabad. Different strategy is required for existing marketplaces to support them as livelihood generation spaces for the poor. People in Centre, after working on Manek Chowk and Teen Darwaja markets, collaborated with SEWA to work on other marketplaces. Few of the engagements were redeveloping marketplaces near Danapith (Rajnagar), State Transport (ST) bus-stand area and Jamalpur vending markets accommodating existing street sellers. The basic plans can be [viewed here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B-_-328lImDicGpZUjVfSEdLQUU/view?usp=sharing).
