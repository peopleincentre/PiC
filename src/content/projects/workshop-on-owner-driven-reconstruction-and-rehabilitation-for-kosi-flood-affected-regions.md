ODR Collaborative along with Government of Bihar organised this workshop in Patna in December 2008 to discuss rehabilitation of Kosi flood affected region and communities. This workshop also resulted in a policy initiative by Government of Bihar to take up owner driven reconstruction perspective and approach for the forthcoming programme for rehabilitation. The report of the workshop is [available here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/bihar_report.pdf) with due acknowledgement to ODR Collaborative.
