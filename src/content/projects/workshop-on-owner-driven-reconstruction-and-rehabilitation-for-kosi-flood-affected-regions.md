The ODR Collaborative, along with the Government of Bihar, organised this workshop in Patna in December 2008 to discuss the rehabilitation of the Kosi flood-affected region and its communities. The workshop resulted in a policy initiative by the Government of Bihar to adopt an owner-driven reconstruction perspective and approach for the forthcoming rehabilitation programme. The report of the workshop is [available here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/bihar_report.pdf), with due acknowledgement to the ODR Collaborative.
