People in Centre supported the development of technical guidelines and training materials for safer reconstruction in earthquake-affected areas of Afghanistan. The work focused on improving the seismic performance of traditional construction systems while retaining local knowledge, materials and building practices.

The guidance covered adobe construction, pakhsa (cob), stone masonry in mud mortar, timber roofs, domical vaults and jack-arch construction. Training programmes were conducted for engineers, facilitators and field teams to support quality implementation and community engagement.

The programme combined technical guidance with socio-technical facilitation, recognising that resilient reconstruction depends not only on engineering solutions but also on informed owner-builders, skilled artisans and local institutions.
