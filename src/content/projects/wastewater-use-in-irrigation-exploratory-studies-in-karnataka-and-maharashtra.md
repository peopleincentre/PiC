Use of urban wastewater for agriculture in peri-urban and rural farmlands is an idea that is being increasingly becoming relevant. PiC with support from IWMI-Tata Policy Program undertook exploratory studies to understand extent of its use, perceptions of the farmers and associated economics. The studies were published as research highlights under IWMI-Tata Policy program and are available [here](http://www.iwmi.cgiar.org/iwmi-tata/PDFs/iwmi-tata_water_policy_research_highlight-issue_04_2016.pdf) and [here](http://www.iwmi.cgiar.org/iwmi-tata/PDFs/iwmi-tata_water_policy_research_highlight-issue_09_2016.pdf)
