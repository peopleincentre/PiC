The use of urban wastewater for agriculture in peri-urban and rural farmlands is an idea that is increasingly becoming relevant. People in Centre, with support from the IWMI-Tata Policy Programme, undertook exploratory studies to understand the extent of its use, farmers' perceptions and the associated economics. The studies were published as research highlights under the IWMI-Tata Policy Programme and are available [here](http://www.iwmi.cgiar.org/iwmi-tata/PDFs/iwmi-tata_water_policy_research_highlight-issue_04_2016.pdf) and [here](http://www.iwmi.cgiar.org/iwmi-tata/PDFs/iwmi-tata_water_policy_research_highlight-issue_09_2016.pdf).
