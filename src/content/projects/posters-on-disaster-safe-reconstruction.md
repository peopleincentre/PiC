After the tsunami, a set of posters for safe construction was prepared by Vivek Rawal and Dinesh Prajapati while working with UNNATI. Inculcating knowledge and skill of disaster-safe construction in communities is a critical area for intervention, a thrust area also emphasised by the Hyogo Framework for Action. With due acknowledgement to UNNATI, this set of four posters is shared for wider use.
