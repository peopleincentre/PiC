As part of the ODR Collaborative's support to the Kosi Reconstruction and Rehabilitation Programme of the Government of Bihar, People in Centre worked with Hunnarshala Foundation to strengthen the capacities of supervisor artisans and engineers appointed by the Government of Bihar. More than 300 supervisor artisans were trained on technical guidelines to reach out to over 30,000 house owners, with the training covering all stages and details of construction of both bamboo and brick houses. Photographs of these training programmes can be seen in [the photo gallery](https://picasaweb.google.com/101554552104048172409/TrainingOfArtisansInBihar#slideshow/5578243849522707282).
