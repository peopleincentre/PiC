PiC, as part of ODRC support to Kosi Reconstruction and Rehabilitation Programme, Government of Bihar, worked to strengthen capacities of supervisor artisans and engineers appointed by the GoB to provide technical guidance to flood-affected house owners. ‘People in Centre’ Consulting and Hunnashala Foundation built capacities of more than 300 supervisor artisans on technical guidelines to reach out to more than 30000 house owners. Training programmes covered all the stages and details of construction of bamboo as well as brick houses. Some photos of these training programmes can be seen in [the photo gallery](https://picasaweb.google.com/101554552104048172409/TrainingOfArtisansInBihar#slideshow/5578243849522707282).
