The “Livelihood Enhancement of Tribal Families through Sustainable Agriculture and Enterprise Development in Backward Regions of Gujarat” (LEPNRM) programme is supported by **Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiative (APPI)**. The project has been implemented by Development Support Centre (DSC) in 26 remote tribal villages of Meghraj Block, Arvalli district in Gujarat. The project aimed to improve livelihoods of the marginal farmers through sustainable practices and collective marketing by forming a producers company. **People in Centre** conducted the Project End Evaluation with mandate from the APPI so that next phase of the initiative could include the learning for scaling up.
