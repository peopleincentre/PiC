The 'Livelihood Enhancement of Tribal Families through Sustainable Agriculture and Enterprise Development in Backward Regions of Gujarat' (LEPNRM) programme is supported by the **Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiative (APPI)** and has been implemented by the Development Support Centre (DSC) in 26 remote tribal villages of Meghraj Block, Arvalli district, Gujarat. The project aimed to improve the livelihoods of marginal farmers through sustainable practices and collective marketing by forming a producers' company. **People in Centre** conducted the Project End Evaluation with a mandate from APPI so that the learning could be incorporated in the next phase of the initiative for scaling up.
