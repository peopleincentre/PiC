People in Centre published the booklet 'Intermediate Semi-Permanent Shelters in Post Disaster Reconstruction – Need for Change in Mindset', authored by Vivek Rawal, Rajendra Desai and Rupal Desai and published by UNNATI – Organisation for Development Education. The book is based on past experiences in different disasters in India. It is available with the Library of Congress (LCCN 2009346990) via this [permalink](http://lccn.loc.gov/2009346990), and for download [here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/interim_shelter.pdf).
