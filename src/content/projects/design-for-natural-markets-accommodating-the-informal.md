Rushank Mehta of People in Centre was invited to contribute a paper to a themed issue of the journal '[Built Environment](http://www.alexandrinepress.co.uk/built-environment/marketplaces-urban-development-strategy)' on '[Marketplaces as an Urban Development Strategy](http://spatialplanningtudelft.eu/?p=2294)'. Focusing on the themes of 'marketplaces and community' and 'marketplaces and design', the paper analysed the interrelations between different occupant groups of the market and the complex interactions critical for livelihood generation and the smooth functioning of the market. People in Centre proposed a schematic design accommodating all the activities and occupant groups of a natural market, demonstrating that inclusive planning supporting existing interrelations is possible — in contrast to the common solution of removing natural markets. [The paper](http://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/alex/benv/2013/00000039/00000002/art00009), co-authored with Chintan Gohil, can be [viewed here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B-_-328lImDiVDBFaDgxVnhtalU/view?usp=sharing).
