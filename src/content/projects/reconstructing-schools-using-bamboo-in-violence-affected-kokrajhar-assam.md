**People in Centre** collaborated with UNNATI to support local communities in reconstructing the schools destroyed in the ethnic violence in Kokrajhar, Assam in July 2012. People in Centre brought together local artisans with artisans from the Kosi flood-affected region to share their skills and knowledge of bamboo and rebuild new schools. In the process of construction, bamboo trusses and improved bamboo joinery were demonstrated and shared. A total of six schools were constructed for both the Bodo and Muslim communities. Photographs of the schools are available on request.
