Revisiting the question of the impact of development projects on the poor, a study was carried out on the BRTS route while the first phase of the project was being implemented. A detailed survey of street vending activities was carried out under the guidance of Rushank Mehta by the SEWA Grassroots research team, and the report was compiled with the help of student trainees Anuj Bhasin and Shikha Patel from the Nirma University Institute of Law. The study can be [viewed here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B-_-328lImDiSE4wUGpuZFVrNXc/view?usp=sharing).
