Revisiting the idea of impact of the development projects on the poor, another study was carried out on BRTS route while the first phase of the project was being implemented. A detailed survey of street vending activities was carried out under the guidance of Rushank Mehta by SEWA Grassroots research team and report was compiled with help of student trainees Anuj Bhasin and Shikha Patel from Nirma University Institute of Law. The study can be [viewed here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B-_-328lImDiSE4wUGpuZFVrNXc/view?usp=sharing).
