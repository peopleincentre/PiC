After the flash floods of Kedarnath,
 ‘People in Centre’
 team supported the District Administration, Chamoli and Karnaprayag Municipality by preparing GIS based maps for undertaking disaster preparedness and development planning in the town area. As hill towns face scarcity of buildable and safe land, this exercise was undertaken to prepare landuse plan, sensitive zone plan and slope analysis of the town area which can form the base for any planning initiative. The report and maps are available on request.
