‘People in Centre’
 team has made a quick analysis of damage due to unprecedented disaster in Uttarakhand based on available satellite imageries. The effort is to understand the situation and strengthen ecological principles for planning and reconstruction. The assessment study led by Teja Malladi is available for [download here](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/Uttarakhanddisaster.pdf). Furthermore, we made a preliminary visit to Uttarkashi town to understand the devastation. The observations and our analysis in Uttarkashi town can be [downloaded here.](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/Uttarkashitown.pdf)
