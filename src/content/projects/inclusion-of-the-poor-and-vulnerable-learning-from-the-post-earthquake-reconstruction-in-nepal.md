The **National Reconstruction Authority**, Government of Nepal organised a **National Symposium on Nepal Reconstruction (NSNR)** from August 24 to August 27, 2020. Vivek Rawal made this presentation on behalf of UNDP, Nepal. The presentation shares an analysis of the effectiveness of post-earthquake reconstruction policies in enabling the poor and the vulnerable to rebuild their houses. The presentation can be viewed on request.
