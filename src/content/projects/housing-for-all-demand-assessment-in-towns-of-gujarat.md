People in Centre undertook housing demand surveys in the towns of Dehgam, Manasa and Chaklasi under the government's social housing programme — Housing for All. With support from the respective municipalities, the demand assessment and potential site identification process was carried out as per the procedures of the Chief Minister Housing Scheme and PMAY. Other consortium partners are also engaged: Hunnarshala is working in Bhachau and Rapar, and Dustudio is working in Jam Khambaliya.
