People in Centre has undertaken housing demand survey in towns of Dehgam, Manasa and Chaklasi under the government social housing program – Housing for All. With support from respective municipalities, demand assessment and potential site identification process has been undertaken as per the procedures of Chief Minister Housing Scheme and PMAY. Other consortium partners Hunnarshala is working in Bhachau and Rapar. Dustudio is working in Jam Khambaliya.
