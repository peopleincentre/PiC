People in Centre collaborated with NIUA, Biome, and Megh Pyne Abhiyan to undertake a research study on practices of sustainable urban sanitation in India. Nine cities from Gujarat, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamilnadu, and Jharkhand were studied and learnings were presented in a report. People in Centre led the research in Gujarat cities in particular. The report can be downloaded from [this link here](https://scbp.niua.org/sites/default/files/Biome%20Report%20for%20Design_10-1_1.pdf).
