After newspaper reports on traffic obstructions caused by vending activities near Jamalpur Vegetable market, police took harshly on the vendors selling vegetables outside the APMC market. **People in Centre,** along with SEWA worked with the street vendors to organise the marketplace. Several representations were made with the AMC officials to come to agreement for a case for organised vending market under the newly constructed flyover bridge at Jamalpur cross roads. A first draft proposal incorporating the new development in the area can be [viewed here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B-_-328lImDicGpZUjVfSEdLQUU/view?usp=sharing).
