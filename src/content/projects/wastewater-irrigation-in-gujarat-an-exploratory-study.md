People in Centre, in collaboration with the International Water Management Institute (IWMI), undertook an exploratory study to understand the extent of wastewater use for irrigation purposes in peri-urban areas of major cities in Gujarat. To get the full report, [click here](https://badal.peopleincentre.org/s/c73TDzazWLd8aBE).

The highlight of the study was presented at the IWMI-Tata Policy Research Program Annual Partners' Meet 2012 and is also available for [download](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/2012_Highlight-30.pdf). In the talk presented at the IWMI-TATA Annual Partners' Meet at Anand, Gujarat in November 2012, Alka Palrecha presents information on the use of wastewater for irrigation, taking a detailed look at wastewater irrigation including the extent of irrigation, existing institutionalisation, and emerging relationships between urban and rural stakeholders. The talk can be viewed [here](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eIB5lAqRYOc).
