‘People in Centre’ Consulting in collaboration with International Water Management Institute (IWMI) undertook an exploratory study to understand the extent of wastewater use for irrigation purpose in peri-urban areas of major cities of Gujarat. To get the full report, [click here.](https://badal.peopleincentre.org/s/c73TDzazWLd8aBE)

The highlight of the study was presented at IWMI-Tata Policy Research Program Annual Partners’ Meet 2012 and is also available for [download](http://www.peopleincentre.org/documents/2012_Highlight-30.pdf). In the talk presented at the IWMI-TATA Annual Partners’ meet at Anand, Gujarat in November 2012, Alka Palrecha presents information on the use of wastewater for irrigation.The talk takes a detailed look at wastewater irrigation including the extent of irrigation, existing institutionalization, and emerging relationships of urban and rural stakeholders.The talk can be viewed [here](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eIB5lAqRYOc).
